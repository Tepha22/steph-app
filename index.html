Decision rulesHow we adjust — tap to open

Scale flat 2–3 weeks, photos unchanged

Trim ~150–200 kcal (from carbs/fat, never protein) or add a little easy activity.

Losing faster than ~0.7%/week or strength dropping

Add ~150 kcal back. Too fast costs muscle — the whole point is recomp.

Knee pain trending up week over week

Reduce range/load on the offending lift, lean on the rehab block. Not settling → get assessed in person.

A lift stalls 2 sessions in a row

Add 1–2 sets → push to 0–1 RIR → change rep range → swap to a close variation via the Swaps tab, then come back.
